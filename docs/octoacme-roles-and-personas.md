# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical strategy and stewardship for complex projects. They collaborate with product and engineering teams to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term architecture goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for new features
- Review design proposals for scalability and maintainability
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Support developers in making sound technical trade-offs
- Collaborate with DevOps on deployment and infrastructure needs

### Goals
- Ensure technical decisions align with long-term architecture vision
- Reduce technical debt and maintain code quality
- Enable team to make informed technical trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk assessments and technical guidance in standups
- Mentoring and code review comments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Review designs, mentor on best practices, unblock technical decisions
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and effort implications
- **Project Managers**: Flag technical risks and dependencies
- **QA Lead**: Define testability requirements and test strategy

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality strategy and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They partner with developers and product managers to define and measure quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and test coverage targets
- Design test strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end, manual)
- Review acceptance criteria for testability
- Execute or coordinate QA and acceptance testing
- Advise on release readiness and known issues

### Goals
- Maximize quality and user confidence in releases
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable rapid, safe deployments

### Typical Communication
- QA strategy and test coverage discussions
- Test status updates in standups and syncs
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria and test scenarios

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test design and coverage
- **Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and prioritize test scenarios
- **Project Managers**: Communicate test status and blockers
- **Technical Lead**: Define testability requirements in design phase

---

## DevOps / Deployment Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps engineers build and maintain deployment infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and release automation. They enable teams to deliver reliably and frequently.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage deployment infrastructure and environments
- Automate testing and deployment processes
- Support rollback and incident response
- Work with teams on deployment readiness checklists

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and automated deployments
- Minimize deployment failures and downtime
- Provide observability and monitoring for production systems

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and release coordination
- Infrastructure and pipeline updates in standups
- Incident response and post-mortems

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support deployment integration and troubleshoot pipeline issues
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment windows and communicate timelines
- **Technical Lead**: Ensure infrastructure aligns with architecture decisions
- **QA Lead**: Provide testing environments and deployment validation

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors provide business context, funding, and strategic direction for projects. They make go/no-go decisions and ensure alignment with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics
- Provide funding and resource authorization
- Make escalation decisions and resolve blockers
- Receive high-level status updates
- Champion the project across the organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize business risk

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Executive briefings and decision reviews
- Escalation path for business-level blockers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on business outcomes and prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Review status and decide on resource allocation
- **Developers**: Provide business context for features
- **All roles**: Final decision authority on scope, timeline, and go/no-go decisions

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach (Optional)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies and coach teams on continuous improvement. They help remove blockers and foster a culture of psychological safety and learning. (This role is optional and most relevant for larger or more distributed teams.)

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Coach team on agile practices and mindset
- Remove or escalate blockers that impede team velocity
- Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback
- Protect team focus during sprints

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Continuously improve processes and team health

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all agile ceremonies
- One-on-ones and coaching conversations
- Process improvement discussions in retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partner on planning and risk management
- **All team members**: Remove blockers and coach on practices
- **Leadership**: Escalate systemic impediments
